
# Hardware for Artificial Intelligence (HWAI)

**Research Group at Technische Universität Darmstadt**

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of the **Hardware for Artificial Intelligence (HWAI)** research group at **TU Darmstadt**, led by **Prof. Dr.-Ing. Li Zhang**.

Our research explores new paradigms for artificial intelligence and intelligent computing from the perspective of hardware. We focus on developing efficient AI algorithms, hardware-aware neural network architectures, and high-performance computing systems.

## 🔬 Research Areas

Our research focuses on the following areas:

- Efficient machine learning hardware-software architectures
- Hardware accelerators for AI algorithms and systems
- AI computing with emerging devices, including RRAM (Resistive Random Access Memory) and optical components
- Timing analysis of digital circuits
- Design and optimization of efficient machine learning hardware-software architectures
- Machine learning for Electronic Design Automation (EDA) of integrated circuits and systems
- Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI)
- Neuromorphic computing


For more information, visit our [Research Topics](https://www.etit.tu-darmstadt.de/hwai/research_hwai/research_topics_hwai/index.en.jsp).

Below are some of our research projects and open-source implementations.

| Project | Description |
|---|---|
| [CompressKV](https://github.com/TUDa-HWAI/CompressKV) | KV cache compression for efficient large language model inference |
| [Basis Sharing](https://github.com/TUDa-HWAI/Basis_Sharing) | Cross-layer parameter sharing for large language model compression |
| [NN2Logic](https://github.com/TUDa-HWAI/NN2Logic) | Conversion of neural networks into logic flows for edge computing |
| [DiffLUT-Network](https://github.com/TUDa-HWAI/DiffLUT-Network) | Differentiable LUT-based neural network research |
